# Before going live

- [ ] Verify email and portfolio link are intended to be public.
- [ ] Check the ZIP is the build you actually plan to distribute.
- [ ] Review Privacy policy against final extension behavior, including storage, ChatGPT transfer, and donation QR behavior.
- [ ] Confirm that the website's support contact has permission to be listed publicly.
- [ ] Host the site over HTTPS.
- [ ] Verify public paths `/privacy.html`, `/contact.html`, `/guide.html`, and download link.
- [ ] Enter actual privacy URL in Chrome Web Store dashboard Privacy practices.
- [ ] Update in-extension links to the hosted site when domain is known.
- [ ] Validate responsive layout, keyboard nav, and contact links on mobile.
- [ ] Review and remove any old donation placeholders or personal data from extension assets.


## GCash-only donation checks
- [ ] Scan the QR from the public website and extension in the official GCash app. Confirm the intended recipient before any payment.
- [ ] Review the information revealed by the full receiving card and QR; this becomes publicly accessible once published.
- [ ] If under 18, use an eligible account with a parent or guardian involved in managing public donations.
- [ ] Confirm all extension assets and checksum files are from the intended release.
- [ ] Confirm donating is voluntary and all features work without payment.
- [ ] Do not claim this QR is verified as authentic solely from a SHA-256 checksum.
