Optional support

Support QuestionForge with GCash.

QuestionForge is free for teachers and learners. If you'd like to support its continued development, you can make an optional transfer through GCash.

How to donate

  1. Open GCash and choose the QR scanner or upload a saved QR image.
  2. Scan the receiving QR shown here, or save the full card to your phone.
  3. Check the recipient inside GCash and review any fees before confirming.

QuestionForge does not handle payments, collect transaction information, or verify donations. The receiving card may show the recipient's identifying details. Every feature remains free.

Before sending money

Check the payee shown in your GCash app before confirming. If you cannot verify that the recipient is the one you intended to support, cancel the transfer and contact the developer through a trusted channel you already know. A QR on a website can be replaced if the site is compromised.

Donations are never required. QuestionForge will never ask for your GCash PIN, one-time password (OTP), or login details.

What to include in a support request

The fastest way to understand an issue is to know the extension version, Chrome version, what you tried, and what happened. A screenshot with private information removed can also help.

Please don't send student records, private exam answer keys, personal addresses, or confidential teaching documents in a support message.

About QuestionForge

QuestionForge is an independent, free Chrome extension built to help teachers and students prepare, organize, review, and export assessments. It has no required subscription and no built-in AI API.
