Our approach to privacy

QuestionForge is a Chrome side-panel extension that helps users prepare assessments from question text and teaching materials. QuestionForge does not require an account or operate a developer-owned cloud service that receives your lesson files. The extension performs supported document extraction locally in your browser.

This policy describes the behavior of QuestionForge v0.8.4 as of November 15, 2026. It applies to the extension and explains the separate behavior of this informational website.

Information the extension processes

QuestionForge may process information that you voluntarily provide: question text, options, answers, school names, subject, titles, formatting preferences, teaching materials, and curriculum information.

Supported local teaching-material sources include DOCX, XLSX, CSV, TXT, and some text-based PDF files. The original imported files and extracted teaching content are not saved as persistent QuestionForge drafts.

When you deliberately choose Add selection to QuestionForge from a webpage's context menu, only the selected text is transferred through temporary Chrome session storage. This action is not intended to collect your browsing history.

What is saved in your browser

When Save drafts in this browser is enabled, the extension stores question drafts, answer keys, document formatting settings, and prompt preferences using Chrome's local extension storage. This storage is not encrypted by QuestionForge.

The default expiration period for inactive drafts is 30 days, checked when the extension next opens. You may choose 7, 30, or 90 days, no automatic expiration, or turn off draft saving. You can clear locally stored extension data through Privacy & security.

Clearing extension data does not delete Word, PDF, or JSON files already downloaded to your computer, or material copied into another application.

What leaves your browser

QuestionForge does not automatically upload your imported documents, assessments, or local drafts to a developer server. Copy actions happen when you select them.

If you copy teaching material or prompts and paste them into an AI chatbot or another AI service, that service receives the pasted content and processes it according to its own terms and privacy policy. The extension provides a configurable pre-copy warning.

Please remove student names, contact details, grades, and other unnecessary sensitive information before sharing documents with any third-party service.

GCash donations

QuestionForge displays a locally bundled GCash Receive Money QR code for optional support; its public website also offers the receiving QR and full card for viewing and downloading. The extension itself does not process payments, verify transactions, or receive transaction records. Payments take place through GCash separately; scanning the QR may reveal recipient information.

Donations are entirely optional and do not unlock or restrict features.

How this website operates

The QuestionForge information website is a static website. It contains no account registration, contact form backend, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or website-set tracking cookies. The website loads the Instrument Sans typeface from Google Fonts; visiting a page may send technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to Google for that font request. The site uses a small local script for mobile navigation and does not store your activity.

Its hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses and access logs when it serves the site. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub's own privacy policies and infrastructure practices also apply. Clicking an external link or opening your email client may share data with those services.

Permissions and limitations

QuestionForge requests browser permissions for the Chrome side panel, user-triggered context menus, and extension storage. It uses input-size and decompression limits and a Content Security Policy. These safeguards reduce risk but do not guarantee perfect security or extraction accuracy.

PDF documents with complex layout, unusual font encoding, or scanned images may not extract correctly. Teachers should verify every imported source and generated assessment before use.

You stay in control

  • Choose which teaching files or webpage selections to import.
  • Disable local draft saving or change its expiration period.
  • Clear saved QuestionForge extension data.
  • Choose whether to paste source content into any third-party service.
  • Decline optional donations or dismiss reminders.
  • Uninstall the extension at any time.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when QuestionForge's data-handling behavior changes. The effective date will be revised accordingly. This policy must be reviewed against any future released version.

For privacy inquiries, email hutchiejn@gmail.com or visit the Contact page.
