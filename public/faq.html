01 / ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW

Local-first client mechanics.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common setup, privacy, document import, and exam formatting questions.

01Does QuestionForge cost anything?+

No. QuestionForge is free to use. Optional GCash support is available on the Contact page, but donating is completely voluntary and all features remain free.

02Do I need ChatGPT Plus or an AI API?+

No AI API key is needed by QuestionForge. You can use a separate AI chat service to generate the content. That service has its own usage and input limits.

03Does QuestionForge upload my lesson plans?+

Not automatically. Imported files are processed locally in your browser. If you copy extracted material and paste it into ChatGPT or another service, you share it with that service.

04Can I upload scanned PDFs?+

The current extension does not automatically perform OCR. It can fail on scanned or complex PDFs. Prefer selectable-text PDFs or editable DOCX/XLSX versions when possible.

05Can I use a BOW and TOS together?+

The prompt workflow supports curriculum documents and source-based planning. Review each extraction and resolve differing allocations before approving the coverage plan.

06How are my questions and answers saved?+

If draft saving is enabled, drafts and settings are saved in Chrome extension storage without QuestionForge-managed encryption. You can disable saving, change retention, or clear data.

07What formats can I export?+

An editable Word (.docx) document or a print-ready page that Chrome can save as a PDF. The document layout supports margins, columns, custom page details, and answer keys.

08Why does the number of imported questions look wrong?+

ChatGPT may output answer-key tables or headings that confuse plain-text parsing. Prefer structured JSON, check the Review screen, and use the formatting repair prompt if needed.

09Why does the extension ask me to send materials in parts?+

Large documents may exceed the size of a single message. QuestionForge divides verified source content into packets. External AI conversation limits still apply; verify its coverage plan.

10Is this an official ChatGPT or Google extension?+

No. QuestionForge is an independent extension and is not affiliated with OpenAI or Google.

11How do I contact the developer?+

Use the Contact page to email support or open the linked developer GitHub profile.
