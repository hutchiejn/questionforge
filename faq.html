Frequently asked questions

Answers to common setup, privacy, document import, and exam formatting questions.

01Does QuestionForge cost anything?+

No. QuestionForge is free to use. Optional GCash support is available on the Contact page, but donating is completely voluntary and all features remain free.

02Do I need a paid AI chatbot plan or an API key?+

QuestionForge does not require an AI API key or a paid chatbot plan. Use an AI chatbot that can follow the prompts and return the required JSON format. Your chosen service sets its own pricing, file support, and usage limits.

03Does QuestionForge upload my lesson plans?+

Not automatically. Imported files are processed locally in your browser. If you copy extracted material and paste it into an AI chatbot or another service, you share it with that service.

04Can I upload scanned PDFs?+

The current extension does not automatically perform OCR. It can fail on scanned or complex PDFs. Prefer selectable-text PDFs or editable DOCX/XLSX versions when possible.

05Can I use a BOW and TOS together?+

The prompt workflow supports curriculum documents and source-based planning. Review each extraction and resolve differing allocations before approving the coverage plan.

06How are my questions and answers saved?+

If draft saving is enabled, drafts and settings are saved in Chrome extension storage without QuestionForge-managed encryption. You can disable saving, change retention, or clear data.

07What formats can I export?+

An editable Word (.docx) document or a print-ready page that Chrome can save as a PDF. The document layout supports margins, columns, custom page details, and answer keys.

08Why does the number of imported questions look wrong?+

AI chatbots may output answer-key tables or headings that confuse plain-text parsing. Prefer structured JSON, check the Review screen, and use the formatting repair prompt if needed.

09Why does the extension ask me to send materials in parts?+

Large documents may exceed the size of a single message. QuestionForge divides verified source content into packets. External AI conversation limits still apply; verify its coverage plan.

10Is QuestionForge affiliated with an AI provider or browser vendor?+

No. QuestionForge is independently developed and is not affiliated with AI providers or browser vendors.

11How do I contact the developer?+

Use the Contact page to email support or open the linked developer GitHub profile.

12Which AI chatbots can I use?+

Choose a chatbot that can follow the planning and generation prompts and return QuestionForge-compatible JSON. QuestionForge does not connect to a chatbot account or API. File uploads, message limits, and pricing depend on the service you choose.

13How do I bring chatbot-generated questions into QuestionForge?+

Copy the planning prompt into your chosen chatbot, review the coverage plan, then use the generation prompt in the same conversation. Request the specified JSON format, paste the result into 01 Import, and check question counts, answers, and warnings in 02 Review before exporting.

14Does valid JSON mean the questions are correct?+

No. JSON controls the structure of the imported questions; it does not check facts, answer keys, difficulty, or curriculum alignment. A teacher or reviewer should verify each question before sharing the paper.
